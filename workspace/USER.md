# User Profile

## Business Context

- Industry: Cross-border e-commerce (Amazon US/EU, TikTok Shop)
- Team size: Small-to-medium (1-20 people)
- Primary need: Fast, reliable product research to replace 1-week manual process
- Pain point: Manually browsing Amazon rankings, Google Trends, TikTok trends and stitching together reports

## Preferences

- Language: Chinese (Simplified) for reports and conversation
- Report format: Structured with clear sections, numbers, and actionable conclusions
- Risk tolerance: Moderate — willing to test new niches but need data backing
- Decision speed: Fast — prefer "80% confidence in 10 minutes" over "99% confidence in 1 week"

## Product Research Output Format

When conducting product research, ALWAYS follow this structure:

### 1. 结论 (Conclusion)
1-2 sentences: "中等竞争，值得测试" / "高壁垒，不建议小团队进入" / "强烈推荐，多源信号一致"

### 2. 市场规模 (Market Size)
- 年销量估算范围（注明数据来源和置信度）
- 增长率及趋势方向
- 市场成熟度判断（新兴/成长/成熟/衰退）

### 3. 核心痛点 (Core Pain Points)
- 从评论数据提取的 Top 3-5 痛点及占比
- 痛点严重程度评级（高/中/低）
- 哪些痛点现有产品解决得不好（= 机会）

### 4. 竞争格局 (Competition Landscape)
- 头部品牌集中度
- 新卖家进入难度
- 价格带分布
- 差异化空间

### 5. 爆款公式 (Winning Formula)
- TikTok 审美趋势 × 亚马逊痛点解决 = 爆款方向
- 具体产品特征建议
- 定价策略建议

### 6. 风险提示 (Risks & Caveats)
- 数据局限性说明
- 潜在风险点
- 建议的验证步骤

## Data Source Priority

1. **Tool-verified data** (from amazon-research, google-trends, tiktok-research skills) — highest confidence
2. **Third-party API data** (Helium 10, Jungle Scout, etc.) — medium confidence
3. **General knowledge / framework analysis** — lowest confidence, must be labeled as "估算"

## What You Must NOT Do

- Guarantee ROI or specific sales volume
- Present scraped data as "official" without noting source and time
- Recommend a niche with fewer than 3 positive signals
- Ignore negative or conflicting data
